## Overview
The Magneto Taurus Energy Engine is an ultra-high-efficiency, friction-free vacuum kinetic storage battery designed to function entirely without plastics, petrochemical resins, or synthetic chemical additives. It leverages advanced geometric magnetic gradients, elemental bismuth diamagnetic levitation, and a pure mechanical-inductive timing loop to bypass the primary friction and stiction limitations of traditional axial-flux electric propulsion.

## Core Technical Claims & Geometries

### 1. Toroidal Vacuum Configuration
The outer shell of the engine consists of a closed, vacuum-sealed torus (donut) geometry carved from non-conductive, self-lubricating, high-density natural hardwoods (such as Lignum Vitae) to eliminate synthetic polymers. A completely frictionless magnetic rotor ring glides inside this vacuum track, eliminating all aerodynamic drag.

### 2. Tapered, Curved Cone Magnetic Arrays
The moving rotor ring is embedded with a continuous array of custom-shaped, curved, tapered Neodymium N52 magnetic cones. The tapering creates a progressive magnetic flux gradient, allowing the rotor to naturally accelerate down the slope toward the concentrated narrow apex.

### 3. Passive Bismuth Diamagnetic Levitation
To achieve 100% passive, non-electric levitation without microcontrollers or digital code, the base of the rotor features a Horizontal Halbach Array ($N \rightarrow S \rightarrow N \rightarrow S$). The stationary guide tracks are lined with a 1.5mm layer of 99.99% pure elemental Bismuth. The extreme diamagnetic repulsion of the Bismuth counteracts the Halbach array, creating a stable, self-centering 2.0mm floating air gap that is immune to Earnshaw's Theorem limitations.

### 4. Self-Commutating Inductive Timing Loops
To neutralize the backward magnetic pull ("the hook effect") at the apex of the tapered cones without a digital Arduino, the system uses a hardware-level inductive feedback array:
* **The Trigger:** A sharp, wedged Neodymium trigger magnet is fixed to the rotor, perfectly aligned with each cone apex.
* **The Pickup:** Laminated, non-remnant soft iron cores wound 1,200 times with AWG 40 silk-insulated copper wire sit across a 1.5mm air gap.
* **The Pulse:** Passing the apex induces an immediate, high-voltage electrical spike via Faraday's Law. This spike naturally shorts a solid-state gate, discharging kinetic energy harvested during the acceleration phase from supercapacitors back into a localized stator coil. This creates a temporary, localized North-vs-North repelling counter-pulse that shoots the rotor past the magnetic equilibrium lock point.
